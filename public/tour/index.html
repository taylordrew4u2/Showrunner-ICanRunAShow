FAQ

Questions producers ask.

Do my performers need an account?

No. Performers, signers and audience members only ever open a link. Only the producer has an account.

What happens if the venue has no signal?

Install the app to your home screen and it keeps running offline. Your edits are saved on the device and synced when you're back online.

Does it play music from Spotify?

No — you upload the actual audio files for walk-ons and DJ tracks. That means no ads mid-show, no buffering, and no surprise "this song isn't available".

Is it on the App Store?

It runs in any browser and installs to the home screen on iPhone and Android in two taps. Open icanrunashow.com in Safari or Chrome and choose "Add to Home Screen".

Who can see my shows?

Only you. Your data is encrypted on your device with a key made from your password before it's saved. That also means we can't recover it if you forget your password — so download a backup from Settings.

Can I get my data out?

Yes, any time. Settings → export gives you a full backup file of every show, performer and setting.
